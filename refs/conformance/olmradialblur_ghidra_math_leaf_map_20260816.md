## Conclusions

1. No direct caller is missing: the apparent `log2f` xref gap is a Ghidra symbolization issue, resolved by the direct IAT call at `0x180009a29` and the checked-in decompile.
2. `log2f` is structurally specialized away on Mac with native evidence for its only argument. `sin/cos`, `expf`, and especially `atan2f` remain platform-math boundaries rather than globally closed functions.
3. The strongest native evidence is the pinned Strength-290 `log2f/atan2f` return. Because native-derived exported RAW still differs from the Mac typed owner, that tuple's existing fail-close policy is justified and should not be relaxed from leaf evidence alone.
4. A future finite closure should capture unique RadialBlur `sin/cos/expf/atan2f` argument keys from each admitted parameter family under one pinned UCRT identity, then replay final typed output. Capturing isolated values without final-output replay is insufficient.
